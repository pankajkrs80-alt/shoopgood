Shipping & Delivery Policy

At ShopGood, we pride ourselves on fast, in-house manufacturing. All personalized orders are engraved and dispatched from our workshop within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation.

Standard delivery takes between 3 to 7 business days depending on your location within India. Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking link via SMS/Email to monitor your package in real-time.

Please ensure your delivery address and postal code are accurate during checkout to avoid delays. ShopGood is not responsible for packages delivered to incorrect addresses provided by the customer.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancellations: Because our products are highly personalized, we begin the design and engraving process very quickly. You may cancel your order for a full refund within 12 hours of placing it. After 12 hours, cancellations are not accepted.

Returns & Refunds: Custom-engraved jewelry cannot be resold. Therefore, we do not offer returns or refunds for buyer's remorse or customer spelling errors. Please double-check your text and photo uploads before purchasing!

Defective Items: If your item arrives damaged, defective, or engraved incorrectly due to an error on our part, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photo evidence. We will gladly send a free replacement or issue a full refund to your original payment method.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. ShopGood collects only the personal data necessary to fulfill your order, including your name, shipping address, phone number, and uploaded photos/text.

Payments: All financial transactions are handled securely via Razorpay. We do not store or have access to your credit card, debit card, or UPI PIN details.

Authentication: We use Google Firebase for secure OTP-based mobile login. Your phone number is strictly used for order tracking, delivery updates, and secure account access. We do not sell your personal data to third-party marketing agencies.
